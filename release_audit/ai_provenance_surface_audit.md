# AI / Internal Workflow Provenance Surface Audit

Prepared: 2026-09-06
Scope: the entire permanent repository tree (1608 files on disk, of which the
staged public set is a subset). Nothing was excluded from the scan.

## Purpose and non-purpose

This audit checks that the public repository does not accidentally expose
internal AI-assistant prompts, conversation transcripts, agent handoff files, or
assistant-specific workflow instructions.

It is **not** a claim that no AI tooling was used in developing this software,
and it does not attempt to make one. Legitimate scientific code, configuration,
and evidence were not removed on the basis that AI tools may have assisted their
development. Provenance is not falsified in either direction.
